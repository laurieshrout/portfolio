## 1.0 Overview

This procedure explains how authorized users can change or reset an account password used to access an internal network access gateway.

Users must change their account passwords every 30 days and cannot reuse previous passwords.

> **Note:** Account naming conventions and organization-specific identifiers have been removed from this sanitized version.

## 1.1 Methods to Change Passwords

Depending on the situation, there are two ways to update an account password:

1. Change the password before it expires.
2. Reset the password when it is expired or unknown.

For assistance, contact the appropriate internal access-management or system-administration team.

## 2.0 Changing a Password

To change the account password:

1. Open an Internet browser.
2. Navigate to the organization's internal access-gateway website.
3. In **Username**, enter the user name.
4. In **Password**, enter the current password.
5. In the authentication or realm drop-down list, select the appropriate credential option.
6. Click **Sign In**. A Windows Security dialog appears.

   > **Note:** If you are unable to log in, see [3.0 Resetting an Unknown or Expired Password](#30-resetting-an-unknown-or-expired-password).

7. Select the appropriate certificate-based authentication option.
8. Click **OK**. The Welcome page appears.
9. Click **Preferences**. The Preferences page opens.
10. From the Preferences page, select the **General** tab. The **Change Password** option appears.
11. In **Old Password**, enter the current password.
12. In **New Password**, enter the new password.

   > **Password requirements:** New passwords must be at least 12 characters long and must contain characters from at least three of the following categories:
   > - Uppercase letters
   > - Lowercase letters
   > - Numeric digits
   > - Non-alphabetic characters, such as `!`, `$`, `#`, or `%`

13. In **Confirm Password**, re-enter the new password.
14. Click **Change Password**. A confirmation message appears on the page.

> **Note:** Do not change unrelated display settings while changing the password.

## 3.0 Resetting an Unknown or Expired Password

If the account password is unknown or expired, contact the appropriate internal access-management or service-desk team to request a password reset.
